Tactics is a real-time strategy game on a hex grid. It's about economy first and armies second. Every soldier is the end of a production chain you have to build: logging camps feed sawmills, iron and coal mines feed furnaces, furnaces feed blacksmiths, and blacksmiths turn out the shields, pikes and bows your warbands need.

Soldiers also need rations, and each unit type eats something different. Shock troopers need berries, archers need fish, shieldbearers and pikemen need meat, and cavalry and onagers need bread. What the map gives you shapes the army you can field: fish near your start pushes you toward archers, and roaming herds mean meat. Gems for elemental upgrades sit in the contested centre of the map.

There are seven warbands, from workers to onagers, with a matchup table that rewards the right counters. The computer opponent plans its economy around what it needs, reads your visible army composition, and picks its fights based on local advantage.
