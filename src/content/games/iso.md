platform: Roblox
status: Prototype
started: 2026-08-08
order: 3
---

Parties fight through a procedurally assembled chain of combat chambers, building synergistic powers along the way, take on bosses, and return to a social sanctuary that holds their permanent unlocks.

The goal is readable isometric combat, a choice of path at every room, builds that only last one run, and mastery that carries over between runs.
