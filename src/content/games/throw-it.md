Throw It is 7-on-7 arcade football. You play alongside named bot teammates who run routes, cover receivers and make tackles, so a game always has a full roster no matter how many people are online. Ranked play keeps an Elo rating, and there are custom helmets, generated stadiums and touchdown celebrations.

Next up is a draft between drives. After every score or turnover, each player picks one player card and one ability card, such as a hot route or a ball-hawk corner. Drafted players replace bots for the rest of the game, so the team you have in the fourth quarter is the one you built.
