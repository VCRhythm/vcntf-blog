Every dive is a gamble. Players head down into the Zone to scavenge cursed relics, then race to activate the extraction point before they're overwhelmed. The Zone breathes poison, and toxicity keeps rising, so every second underground is borrowed time.

Between dives there's a hub world: the three-storey Trailhead Registry, waylights out to regional outposts, strangers on the trail asking for favors, named guards sitting on the biggest hoards, and five world bosses waiting to be woken.

Every week brings a new theme that changes the enemies, caves, loot, vendors and a weekly twist, so runs in one week play differently from the next.
